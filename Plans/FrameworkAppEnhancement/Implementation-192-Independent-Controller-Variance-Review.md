## Controller workflow and evidence

An independently authorized controller can open the existing recorded invoice proposal and request a fresh review. The screen identifies the current reviewer, proposal preparer and source creator, shows current reconstructed comparisons and revisions, and distinguishes correction from a possible within-policy variance disposition. It explicitly says that viewing the review records no controller decision, task completion, financial matching, posting or payment. Invoice status remains DRAFT.

The reviewer must differ from both the verified original source creator and recorded proposal preparer. Blank, SYSTEM, oversized or control-containing reviewer identities are rejected. APInvoiceVarianceDecisionReview lives in TheTechIdea.Data, with full current financial evidence, reconstructed evidence, verified recorded proposal, original production binding, review revision and UTC retrieval time. Require verifies the complete addressed company/account/invoice/source revision, proof identity, reconstruction and review digest; altered header, binding, proof or revision cannot be consumed.

RecordedVarianceEvidence extends the existing financial owner. Only the original invoice header is reconstructed from verified persisted proposal proof. Invoice lines, purchase orders, allocations, complete global receipt contributors, receiver decisions and matching policy history remain current. Every reconstructed component is addressed to the current reader; actor-bound commands are not reassigned. The previous OriginalVarianceEvidence exact-retry entry point still requires the original command and preparer before calling this shared reconstruction. Content revision equality across independent readers does not grant authority or fabricate historical source snapshots.

REQUEST_CORRECTION is available for a verified independent review. ACCEPT_WITHIN_POLICY_VARIANCE is only an eligible review outcome when all reconstructed evidence still matches the proposal, effective policy is verified, activity/draft/maker/total/amount/allocation/receipt-acceptance/global-consumption and policy price/quantity checks are confirmed, and all known differences are price differences within verified policy limits. Changed evidence, exceeded quantity/price limits or unknown required diagnostics remove acceptance eligibility. This is limited variance eligibility; tax/non-PO and book/period/journal evidence remain separate and no overall financial approval follows.
