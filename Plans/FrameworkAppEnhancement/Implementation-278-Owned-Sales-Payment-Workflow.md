## Required behavior and personas

1. The accountant reviews an actual posted receivable/book ledger and existing native payment proposals/outcomes. Show original customer, FIELD, original currency/precision, invoice amount, posted references and separate proposed/released totals. Missing ledger or payment evidence is unavailable, not zero inferred from an invoice approval.
2. Capture an explicit payment proposal in AR_PAYMENT with original company/book/FIELD/receivable/currency, unique document/trace references, method, payer, date, positive exact amount, observation/evidence and reason. Do not clamp overpayments or automatically allocate another currency. Retain the original command before mutation and preserve exact native audit/receipt. Proposals reserve only proposed amounts; they do not establish payment, settlement or cash posting.
3. The controller independently reviews the original proposal against current native invoice/ledger/payment/evidence facts. Separate current purpose authority, maker exclusion, explicit dispositions/evidence and durable original request recovery are required. Rejected work returns to its original maker without rewriting prior receipts.
4. A separately authorized release action confirms approved payment and allocation, books/periods/mappings and explicit FX carrying/settlement rates where applicable. Atomically record actual payment/AR allocation and balanced cash/receivable/FX ledger correlation through existing owners in one owned Serializable PRODUCTION transaction. Preserve original source currency, original receipts and posting history. Complete rollback/read-back and exact unknown-commit reconciliation are required; no duplicate or partial repair is allowed.
5. Extend existing API/client/circuit/Mud forms, assigned tasks/process source and accountant/controller/billing landings. Current standard app roles and purpose/FIELD authority remain independently enforced. Keep original request recovery separate from fresh preparation/review/release; hide private evidence after context changes and fence late results. PRODUCTION and LIFECYCLE owned transactions never join.
6. Add actual currency-specific paid/outstanding/settled drill-down only from proven release/allocation/ledger records. Credit, dispute, cancellation/replacement and reversals retain separate controlled history. Verify all named persona actions before closing this phase or the full goal.
