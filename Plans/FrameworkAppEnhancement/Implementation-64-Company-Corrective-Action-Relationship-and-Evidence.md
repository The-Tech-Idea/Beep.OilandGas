## Problem and resulting behavior

Corrective actions previously looked up a derived CA incident ID and inferred its incident relationship from freeform PROJECT_NUM. Its PROJECT and PROJECT_STEP_BA paths had no reviewed row authority. Status could default to OPEN, due date to thirty days after the read, and ambiguous participant/status histories to one arbitrarily selected row. A completed action could pass deadline review even when actually completed late.

The existing owner now resolves an actual CORRECTIVE_ACTION project through the canonical HSE_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.PROJECT_ID foreign key. Current HSE.View permission, inherited eligible FIELD authority and the existing incident primary FIELD/header evidence remain prerequisites. Actual recorded plan IDs are reused; neither an ID format nor a project number establishes scope. Actual status reads require explicit recorded action/status/date/responsible-participant evidence. Deadline review compares actual completion against the recorded due date, including equality. Missing or conflicting facts refuse interpretation.

## Actual corrective-action interpretation and mutation boundary

The actual service requires unique positive integral int-range sequences and exact unique step IDs, recorded action names/types, explicit UTC due dates, a recorded current status with UTC effective date, and one exact responsible assignment per action. It selects a unique latest effective-date status; equal latest timestamps refuse instead of using row-created date. All supplied status/assignment rows must have actual nodes; orphans cannot disappear from counts. Missing participant or status facts cannot become defaults.

COMPLETED requires an actual UTC completion date no later than the review or its recorded completion status. An incomplete status cannot carry completion evidence. Late completed actions remain overdue; AllCAsMeetDeadlineAsync returns false for them. An empty recorded plan can be displayed as empty but cannot establish completion/deadline success. These facts do not certify approval, investigation sufficiency or closure prerequisites.

CreateCAPlanAsync can reuse one proven existing recorded linked plan. A missing plan explicitly refuses because project plus incident-response creation needs an owned compound command/transaction. No unlinked project is inserted. Assignment/due-date/completion targets use the same canonical plan relationship and reject ambiguous/missing sequences. Generic HSE/PROJECT mutations remain fenced and effective authority remains READ; durable allocation, idempotency, transaction/readback/reconciliation and participant assignment commands are future acceptance work.
