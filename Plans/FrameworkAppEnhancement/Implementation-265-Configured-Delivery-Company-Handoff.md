The delivery step requires a native acceptance decision by the acting independent reviewer and current ProductionAccounting.Approve permission, in addition to the assigned standard role and Workflow.Approve. A missing decision, different reviewer, missing current permission, original maker/starter, changed company/FIELD/source or mismatched definition refuses completion. The complete delivery source is reread outside the owned lifecycle transaction; transaction callbacks retain current RBAC/configuration/datasource checks without cross-module business reads.

ROUTE_DELIVERY_REJECTION instead records delivery rejection history and closes its approval task while creating the configured original-preparer review task. Only the original preparation actor with current preparation and workflow-execution permission can complete that review. Completion records DELIVERY_REJECTION_REVIEWED history and terminal REJECTION_REVIEWED state; it does not amend or approve acceptance.
