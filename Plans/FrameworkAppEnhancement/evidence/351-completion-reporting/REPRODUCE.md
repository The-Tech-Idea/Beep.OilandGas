Results are REPORTED_PASS, REPORTED_FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE; none represents independent acceptance or a qualified physical test. Referenced tests may predate spud or follow reported physical completion; applicability belongs to independent review. Future performed/completion UTC, missing required evidence and unreviewed outstanding work refuse. Actual report controls load known observation values, require a current configured role, explicit completion reporting/amendment reason and structured completion/test fields. Dates/results/review are not guessed. Frozen confirmation, actual encrypted retained original and readonly lost-ack recovery remain shared. Explicit withdrawal remains auditable. The assigned-persona page loads current progress/evidence on request, preserves its qualified responsibility during a progress outage, and fences company/persona/route/disposal changes.
